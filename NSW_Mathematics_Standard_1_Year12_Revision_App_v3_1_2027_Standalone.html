Scope and assessment

This is the Year 12 Mathematics Standard 1 course, taught under the 2024 NESA syllabus from Term 4, 2026, for the 2027 HSC cohort. Year 11 is assumed knowledge. These are original practice sets, not official HSC papers or the separate HSC Exam Simulator.

NESA Year 12 Standard 1 school assessment components: Knowledge and understanding 50%, Working mathematically 50%. The optional HSC examination is 80 marks (15 objective + 65 other response), 120 minutes plus 10 minutes reading. Approved calculator and NESA reference sheet permitted. This revision app uses flexible school-style practice sets, not the fixed official HSC blueprint.
