Official examination rules

The examination is worth 70 marks, permits a NESA-approved calculator and provides a reference sheet. Section II contains 23–28 items with at least one 4- or 5-mark item. This simulator uses 25 written items, including two 4-mark items, and does not reproduce previous HSC questions.

Syllabus coverage

The six Year 12 outcomes are required in every standard generated paper. Working mathematically (MAO-WM-01) is assessed across questions. The optional Year 11 assumed-knowledge slot is intentionally small.
