Paper preparation

Section I consists of ten objective-response questions. Section II contains 40 marked response items totalling 90 marks. Topics and question families are varied using a deterministic seed.

The official specification permits 37–42 Section II items; this simulator deliberately uses 40.

During the examination

The initial 10-minute perusal displays the paper but locks responses. After perusal the 3-hour writing clock starts. You may explicitly skip perusal for practice. Written working is self-marked only after submission.
