NESA examination specification and syllabus verification

This application is based on Chemistry Stage 6 Syllabus (2017), including NESA errata for Modules 5–8. The replacement Chemistry 11–12 Syllabus (2025) first applies to the HSC in 2029, not 2027.

The official 2027 Chemistry HSC has 100 marks: Section I objective response (20), Section II written response (80, 20–25 items including at least two worth 7–9 marks). Writing time is 3 hours and reading time is 5 minutes. NESA provides a formulae sheet, data sheet and Periodic Table, and permits NESA-approved calculators. Module weighting is approximately equal across Modules 5–8; Year 11 assumed knowledge and Working Scientifically skills are integrated.
