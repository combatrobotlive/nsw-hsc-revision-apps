Section 03

Generated Practice Assessment

Original revision paper, not an official NESA examination. The 100-mark option follows the published Chemistry HSC layout: 5 minutes reading, 3 hours writing, Section I (20 multiple-choice marks) and Section II (80 written marks, 20 questions including four 8-mark integrated tasks). Modules 5-8 receive equal marks, with relevant Year 11 assumed knowledge. Shorter 40/60/80-mark sets are non-official school-style revision papers.
