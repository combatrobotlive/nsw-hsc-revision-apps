Scope and assessment

This is the common Year 11 Mathematics Standard course (course 11236), suitable for students progressing to Year 12 Standard 1 or Standard 2. It does not include Year 12-only content. Practice exams are independent, generated school-style revision sets—not official NESA examinations.

NESA Year 11 school assessment components: Knowledge and understanding 50%, Working mathematically 50%. Individual schools determine assessment tasks. This app is not an official school assessment schedule.
