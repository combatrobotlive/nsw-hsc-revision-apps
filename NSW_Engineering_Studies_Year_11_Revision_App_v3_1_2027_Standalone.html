STUDY HABITS

Answering Preliminary engineering questions

Identify

Give the relevant name or property, not a lengthy explanation.

Describe

Provide characteristics, features or a sequence of steps.

Explain

Show how or why a principle produces the observed result.

Discuss

Consider different relevant issues, benefits, limitations and consequences.

Calculate

Write the relationship, substitute values, show units and check physical sense.

Graph / draw

Use legible axes, scales, labels, dimensions and third-angle/AS 1100 conventions.

Examinations generated here are original school-style revision sets. They are not NESA-written, and handwritten drawings require human marking.
