Before you begin

  • Have a NESA-approved calculator, the official Mathematics Standard 1 and 2 HSC reference sheet (2024 syllabus), writing paper and suitable drawing equipment.
  • Reading time is read-only. During writing time, record answers and working, and draw graphs or diagrams on paper when required.
  • Responses are saved automatically in this browser. Your timer continues even if you close the page. Answers and marking are unavailable until submission.
  • The simulator awards raw practice marks, not predicted NESA performance bands or scaled HSC results. Mark your own written and method responses against the criteria.
