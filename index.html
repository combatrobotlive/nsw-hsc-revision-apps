How to use the NSW apps

Choose how you want to practise, then check your answers and revise what needs work.

1. Choose the correct course

Select Engineering Studies or Physics and open Year 11 or Year 12 practice. The app displays its applicable NESA syllabus and cohort.

2. Practise original questions

Simple and Complex Practice draw on original question families, calculations and stimulus material. These are revision aids, not replacements for teaching.

3. Try the HSC simulator

Where linked, open the separate timed HSC Exam Simulator. Use exam review and Teacher Tools to focus your next revision session.

Saving progress, teacher tools and official information

App answers are generally saved in your own browser, using each app's local storage. Some apps offer direct exports or a download function. The homepage backup includes localStorage from the current website origin only; it does not include IndexedDB and may include QCAA data when both collections share a GitHub Pages domain. Use the official NESA syllabus site to confirm curriculum and assessment requirements.
