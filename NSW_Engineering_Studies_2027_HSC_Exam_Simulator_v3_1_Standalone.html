What your marker expects

Show working with units, label engineering diagrams, justify material and process choices using service conditions, and answer the command verb. Graphical and tabular stimulus must actually inform the response. Use approved drawing equipment for orthogonal and development tasks.

At least one part of each paper will require student/teacher judgement after submission. Automatic marking applies only to numerical and multiple-choice parts.
